Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Trivisia Interiors ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through trivisiainteriors.com, how we use it and the choices you have. It is written in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Information we collect

When you request a free estimate or consultation, you may share:

  • Your name and phone number
  • Your property type and city
  • Any requirements or messages you choose to include

Our website does not store these details on any server. When you submit the form, your details are placed into a WhatsApp message on your own device, and you decide whether to send it to us.

How we use your information

  • To contact you about your enquiry, consultation, estimate or project
  • To plan, design and carry out work you ask us to do

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

WhatsApp and phone calls

Messages you send us on WhatsApp are handled under WhatsApp's own privacy policy and terms. We keep enquiry conversations only as long as needed to respond to you and manage your project.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. To show the page, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and some images from Unsplash, and our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. These providers handle that data under their own privacy policies.

How we protect your information

The website is served only over encrypted HTTPS. Access to enquiry details is limited to the Trivisia team members who need it to help you.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to be contacted, at any time by calling or messaging us on WhatsApp.
