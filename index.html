FAQ

Questions clients often ask.

Everything you need to know about working with an interior designer in Maharashtra. Still curious? Ask us directly on WhatsApp.

Ask on WhatsApp
How much does interior design cost in Maharashtra?

Cost depends on the size of your space, the scope of work and the materials and finishes you choose. After a free consultation and site visit we share an itemised estimate, so you know exactly what you are paying for, with no hidden costs.

How long does an interior project take?

It depends on the scope: a single room or modular kitchen is much quicker than a full home or office fit-out. We agree a committed timeline with you before work starts and keep you updated at every stage until handover.

Which cities in Maharashtra do you work in?

We work across Maharashtra, including Mumbai, Pune, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Nashik, Nagpur, Aurangabad, Kolhapur and Solapur, with dedicated project management for every site.

Can I see the design before work begins?

Yes. Every project starts with space planning and photo-realistic 3D visualisation. You can walk through the design, request changes and approve it with confidence before any work starts on site.

Do you handle both design and execution?

Yes. Trivisia offers end-to-end turnkey interiors: design, material selection, carpentry, electrical, false ceilings, painting and final styling, all managed by one accountable team.

Do you design offices, shops, cafes and clinics?

Yes. Alongside homes we design commercial interiors including corporate offices, retail showrooms, cafes, clinics and boutiques, built around your brand, workflow and customers.

Do you take up renovation of existing homes?

Yes. We renovate existing homes and offices, from a single kitchen or bedroom makeover to a complete transformation.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. The first design consultation is free and there is no obligation. Share your requirements using the form or on WhatsApp and our team will get in touch.
